Imperi Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Imperi is developed by Summer Lawson, an individual in Columbus, Ohio, USA. This policy describes the current Imperi iPhone app and the separate Imperi website waitlist.

Information Imperi handles

In the iPhone app: If you choose to connect Apple Health, Imperi reads your sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and steps. It uses them on your iPhone to create your morning forecast and to show how your heart rate changes after a reset. It does not write anything to Apple Health, and it does not send health data to a server. The app keeps preferences and activity records on your device, including nudge settings and notification outcomes, check-in answers and times, stress-detection timestamps, reset strategy and rating information, heart rate results from resets, and each day’s forecast with the sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability values behind it. If you set up app pauses, Apple’s Screen Time features handle your selected apps or categories and schedules on your device. Imperi does not send those selections or usage information to its backend. Fuel taps (a meal, snack or glass of water marked for the day, with the time of each tap) are saved as a daily history on your iPhone. They include no amounts or calories.

On the website: If you join the waitlist, the website sends your email address to the AWS services configured for Imperi. It is stored as a waitlist entry so Summer can manage the waitlist and contact you about it. The website and app code reviewed do not show account registration, sign-in, or advertising/analytics tracking. Although email sign-in is configured in the backend, the app and website do not currently use it.

Health information

Imperi reads HealthKit information only if you allow it, and you can change that access at any time in the Health app. Imperi does not write to HealthKit. HealthKit information is used only to provide the Imperi features described above, and it is not sent to Imperi’s backend. Health or HealthKit data is never sold, used for advertising or marketing, or shared with third parties for those purposes.

Storage and protection

App preferences and records are kept in the app’s storage on your iPhone, rather than uploaded to Imperi’s backend. Their protection depends on your device and iOS security settings; the app does not add a separate encryption layer. Imperi excludes these records from iCloud and other iPhone backups, so your Imperi history does not move to a new iPhone; use “Export my data” in Settings to keep a copy. If you set up app pauses, the name of your latest forecast (for example, “Partly cloudy”) is kept in iOS Screen Time settings so the pause screen can show it, and iOS may include that in a backup. Waitlist email is sent to and stored by AWS Amplify/AppSync and DynamoDB. The website connects to the AWS service; the deployed AWS region and specific retention and access settings depend on the live deployment and are not specified in the app source.

AWS processes waitlist email to provide the waitlist service. Imperi does not sell personal information or use it for advertising, and does not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Exporting and deleting information

There is no account in the iPhone app. In Settings, “Export my data” lets you save a copy of what Imperi has stored, and “Delete all my data” removes it from your iPhone. Removing Imperi from your iPhone also removes its active on-device app data; device backups may be governed by your iOS backup settings. Neither changes your data in Apple Health, which you manage in the Health app. To request deletion of a waitlist email, write to imperi.support@gmail.com. There is no self-service waitlist deletion feature at this time. The backend’s email sign-in configuration is not used by the app or website; if you believe you have an account, contact us and we will review the request.

Children

Imperi is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Health and safety

Imperi is a wellness tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional medical care. If you have health concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional. Imperi is not a crisis service. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988 (US) or go to your nearest emergency room.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact Summer Lawson at imperi.support@gmail.com.
